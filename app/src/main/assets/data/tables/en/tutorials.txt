# Contextual tutorials (first line = title). Shown once each, never blocking for long.
@ lentille
The Surveyor's Lens
The "lens" button, top right, wakes the faded marks of the scene for a few seconds: boundary stones, whispers, hidden details.
It is a tool, not a hint: its use is unlimited and part of the world.
@ explore
Exploring
Tap a point of interest to examine, listen, take or talk. Tap an arrow to move to another scene.
Some points ask you to stay: hold your finger to wait, to hear an echo or to let a tide pass.
One of Lohen's thoughts appears at the bottom: tap it to close it.
@ carnet
The notebook
The "notebook" button opens the double-page notebook: map of the peninsula, satchel, Esteban's pages, echoes, characters, log of puzzles and secrets.
The map in the notebook also lets you travel between known areas.
@ sacoche
The satchel
Objects go into the satchel (a tab of the notebook). Each object carries three thoughts: on the first, the second and the tenth examination.
There is no money in this game: the currency is favours.
@ filou
Asking Filou
Filou does not speak. He reacts: Lohen translates, with her margin of error. Tap "Ask Filou" to question him about what surrounds you, or tap him to pet him.
He always knows first and can never say it.
@ filou_join
Filou walks with you
Filou follows Lohen everywhere. He will stop by himself where he does not want to go: that will be a silent sentence, not an error.
@ gold
Choices in gold ink
Options marked with a golden star are final: the world will remember them. No choice is "wrong": each path gives a different colour to the same scenes.
@ puzzle
Puzzles
Everything needed to solve a puzzle is on the screen or in the notebook. There is no failure and no timer: only states of searching.
The "?" button offers hints on three levels, always at your request. The "×" button closes the puzzle: you can come back.
@ bornes
The surveyor's stones
Forty engraved boundary stones mark the peninsula. Each stone read with the lens adds a survey to the notebook and a point of Clarity.
Three hands engraved them: Aurélien Vasseur, Ombeline, and an unknown hand.
@ epilogue
The answer
This choice closes the chapter. You may write a free answer with the keyboard: it is kept in your save, on this device only, and is sent nowhere.
After the end, the chapter stays open as a "free memory": nothing is lost.
